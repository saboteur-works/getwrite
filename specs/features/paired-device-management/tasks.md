## Open Questions
- Corrupt store with sharing off: the spec's visibility rule (FR-20) covers "sharing enabled or in effect, or the store holds at least one device". A corrupt store has no readable device count, and today `SharingSettings.tsx` shows `SHARING_STORE_CORRUPT` only when sharing is enabled or in effect. Task 9 keeps that existing behaviour (nothing extra shown with sharing off and a corrupt store). Whether the unreadable message and file location should also show with sharing off is not decided here; the owner decides.
- Poll interval and date format: the spec leaves the poll interval to the task (Task 9) and does not fix the date and time format (Task 7); both are chosen and recorded by the implementor and reviewed by the owner at the exercise stage. Not a spec question, listed so the report is expected.
